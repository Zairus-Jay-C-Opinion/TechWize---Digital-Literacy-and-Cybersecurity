What is   Cyber Safety ?

with techwize.

TechWize empowers you to become a confident and responsible technology user. Master essential digital skills, explore productivity tools, and strengthen your cyber safety practices—all while building the knowledge and wisdom needed to navigate the digital world like a true wise tech-wizard.

Digital literacy is the ability to use digital tools confidently, find and evaluate online information, communicate effectively, and make responsible choices when using technology. It helps people learn, work, and stay safe in a digital world.

Cyber safety is the practice of protecting yourself and your information online. It includes using strong passwords, avoiding scams, keeping personal data private, and making smart, respectful choices on the internet.

Together, we can build a digitally literate and cyber-safe society where everyone knows how to protect themselves online and use technology responsibly.
