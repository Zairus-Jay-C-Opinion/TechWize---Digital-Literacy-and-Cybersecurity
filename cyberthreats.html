Common Cyber Threats

Phishing Attacks

Fraudulent emails, messages, or websites designed to trick you into revealing passwords, credit card numbers, or other sensitive information.

Identity Theft

Criminals steal personal information to commit fraud, open accounts in your name, or access your financial resources without authorization.

Malware & Ransomware

Malicious software that can damage your device, steal data, or lock your files until you pay a ransom to cybercriminals.

Social Engineering

Manipulation tactics used to trick people into breaking security procedures or giving away confidential information through psychological manipulation.
